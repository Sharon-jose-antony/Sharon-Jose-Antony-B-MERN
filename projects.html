1. VigilScan – Antivirus Prototype

VigilScan is a lightweight antivirus-style prototype developed as a cybersecurity project. It uses hash-based signatures to identify files that match known signatures.

The prototype includes file monitoring, a quarantine feature for detected files, and scan logs to record activity. It demonstrates basic concepts used in file-based malware detection.

Technologies: Python, Tkinter, Watchdog, Hashing

2. DanaChain – Need-First Donation Network

DanaChain is a prototype concept for an intelligent donation network. Its goal is to connect available donation resources with individuals and communities based on their needs.

The idea focuses on making donation distribution more organised and need-oriented, helping resources reach the people who require them.

Area: Web Development and Social Impact

Planned technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python
